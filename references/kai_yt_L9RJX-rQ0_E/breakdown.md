## Why it flopped
**Seen:**
1. **Letterboxed first frame**: half the screen is black at 0.0s.
2. **No person and no BTS of Kai.** His winners all show the pilot or builder.
3. **No voice and no hook line.** The first word is effectively absent (vs 0.00s on every winner).
4. **No answer to the question it implies.** Two nearly identical results with no verdict.
5. **Tiny labels** that can't be read on a phone.

**Guessed:**
- It's a gear-reviewer format (side-by-side test) without the gear reviewer's payoff (a verdict, a
  surprising loser). Only people in the buying window care, and the title targets them, not Kai's
  audience.
- The quality difference that makes the comparison meaningful is invisible after YouTube compression on
  a 6-inch screen.

## Steal / avoid
1. **Never letterbox frame 1.** Fill 9:16 or punch in.
2. **A comparison needs a verdict** (shown, not just implied) and a surprising one if possible.
3. **Put a person in frame 1**: Kai rigging, driving or reacting.
4. **Exaggerate the difference** (a stress test: low light, bumps, speed) so it's visible on a phone.
5. **Title the outcome, not the model numbers** ("$300 camera beat my $1,000 one").
