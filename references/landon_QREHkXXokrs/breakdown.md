52.1s, 1080×1920 at 23.98fps, −13.2 LUFS (on target). 36 shots, avg 1.45s, 6.7 cuts/10s. VO covers 86%

## Hook (0–3s)
- **Frame 1 (0.0s):** a small kid in a red shirt sitting at a pro editing workstation (Premiere
  timeline on an iMac, 3D-printer filament on the shelf). Sound off: *"why is a 6-year-old at that
  computer?"* An incongruity in one image.
- **First line at 0.00s:** "I gave my six-year-old his own computer, and then I left for a week." It's
  a concrete setup with **built-in danger**, said in 3.3s.
- **First cut at ~1.9s:** a plane in the sky on "left for a week". **Every noun gets a shot.**
- **3.3s:** "Which is a problem, because technically he could access the entire internet." The stakes
  are explicit by 7.5s.
- **Captions: one word at a time**, large white bold sans with a soft shadow, centred at about 55–60%
  height.

## Retention devices
- **Named characters** (Theo, Scout, Simon): each bot is a mini-story beat about every 8–10s.
- **"He's not the only one"** at 21.2s re-opens the loop at the 40% mark.
- **Constant visual change** (36 shots, none longer than 4.1s), with a mix of **phone screens, POV hands,
  selfie-to-camera, nature, car interiors**: casual iPhone texture with occasional clean cinema shots.
- **Self-deprecating, human line** at 38.7s.

## Captions
One word at a time, white heavy rounded sans (Montserrat ExtraBold-like), large (~70px at 1080w), soft
drop shadow, centred mid-frame. Punctuation kept ("computer…", "road,", "trees,"). Keyword CTA in quotes
("BOT"). This is the "Landon style" already in the playbook.

## Pacing
6.7 cuts/10s, steady; VO at 244 wpm with almost no pauses. Fast, but the one-word captions keep it readable.

## Audio
VO-led, light music bed, −13.2 LUFS (on target).

## Ending
An explicit CTA with a keyword comment trigger ("comment BOT"), on a selfie, then it ends ~2s later.

## Platform context
Title: "My Grok Bots have been keeping things moving while I'm on the road." The description continues
the bot list. Uploaded 2026-08-18. A brand deal (Grok).

## Why it worked
- **Seen:** an incongruous first frame; a concrete setup + stakes in under 7.5s; one-word captions;
  every noun illustrated; named characters as re-hooks; a mix of polished and organic phone footage;
  on-target loudness; a clear CTA.
- **Guessed:** a family/kid angle gives broad appeal far outside tech. The view count is probably
  inflated by paid distribution (low like rate, sponsor CTA).

## Steal / avoid
1. **Frame 1 = an incongruity** (something that shouldn't be there) readable sound-off.
2. **The setup sentence carries its own danger** ("…and then I left for a week").
3. **Name your tools or rigs like characters** ("my drone, Clanker") so each becomes a story beat.
4. **Re-open the loop at about 40%** ("but that's not the only one").
5. **Avoid copying its numbers as a target**: it's a sponsored post, so benchmark retention craft, not reach.
