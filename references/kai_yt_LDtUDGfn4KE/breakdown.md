## Why it flopped
**Seen:**
1. **Sequential, not simultaneous.** BTS (0–3.3s) then result; Kai's winners run BTS and result
   **side by side** for the whole video (wQsN3IJga6I from 0.0s, the #1 from 3s).
2. **Frame 1 is a mundane wide shot** (a kart in an empty lot), with the drone barely visible.
3. **No stated stake or claim on screen.** "Impossible" is only in the title; the video never explains it.
4. **No text at all** for sound-off viewers.
5. **The payoff is too subtle:** a tilted chase shot without an obvious "how?!" moment.

**Guessed:**
- "BTS vs final" works when the BTS shows something **surprising about the method** (one take, live
  crowd, a crazy rig). Here the BTS is "a man in goggles in a parking lot", which says nothing about
  why the result is special.
- The Avata 360's selling point (reframing in post) is invisible in the BTS by definition, so the
  comparison has no gap to close.
- Kai expected it to do well because *to a pilot* the shot is hard. The audience isn't pilots.

## Steal / avoid
1. **Keep BTS and result on screen together** (split) for the whole runtime. Don't cut from one to the other.
2. **Say why it's hard in the first 2s** ("No one's done this with a kart at 40 mph").
3. **Frame 1 must contain the drone or the pilot at a readable size.**
4. **Always add hook text** for sound-off viewers.
5. **Avoid product-name titles** unless it's a launch day.
