# Breakdown: "Flying into a $2.5 Million Sushi Restaurant" (IG, Dec 2024)

**Kai's #1 short.** 1.03M likes and 2.1K comments; Instagram hides the view count. 79s,
1080×1920 at 23.98fps, one continuous FPV take. Co-edit and SFX by Roger.

## Hook (0–3s)
- **Frame 1:** a cinematic FPV shot drifting through autumn trees over a storefront street.
  It's pretty, but the scroll-stop comes from the countdown.
- **0.0–2.5s:** captions "3, … 2, … 1, … ACTION!" in a teal italic. A countdown builds
  anticipation instantly: the viewer knows something is about to start and that it's live and
  can't be redone. That's an open loop in 2 seconds with no context needed.
- **About 3.0s:** split screen comes in. FPV on top (~60%), Kai in goggles below (~40%). The
  bottom half shows *it's a real person flying this live*, which turns a pretty drone shot into
  a skill and stakes story.
- **The post caption carries the stakes:** "$2.5 Million Sushi Restaurant" plus "1-Take".

## Structure
| Time | Beat |
|---|---|
| 0–3s | Countdown on full-screen FPV. Anticipation. |
| 3–12s | Entrance into the restaurant; diners toasting ("Cheers!"). |
| 12–37s | Through the kitchen: "fire", "the fish", chefs working. Craft. |
| 37–57s | Sushi bar and the chef up close; guests. People and payoff. |
| 51–60s | Kai's mild mistake: "I think I blew out a lot of stuff there, but it's okay." Human, raises tension. |
| 61–79s | Back out the door, rising to an aerial reveal of the town. "*Crew clapping*". Release. |

## Retention devices
- **One take = constant implied tension** ("will he crash?"). There are zero cuts and it
  still holds for 79s.
- **The split screen is a second story running in parallel:** Kai's directing ("No, don't
  shadow", "Good job, Daiji") is BTS commentary layered over the result.
- **The setting changes every ~10s:** street → dining room → kitchen → bar → street → sky.
  Each new space is the re-hook.
- **The ending pull-out and clapping** give a clear finish and a feeling of success.

## Captions
- A small italic in teal or white, 1–3 words with trailing commas or ellipses, placed on the
  split seam. They read like overheard dialogue, not a transcript.
- Sound-effect labels in asterisks (*Crew clapping*).
- **Not Montserrat.** This is a different, lighter look than the one-word-pop style we plan
  to test. Worth keeping for the FPV split-screen format specifically.

## Audio
Kai's live directing audio carries it; speech covers 44% of the runtime. Exported loudness is
−16.3 LUFS, quieter than the −14 target.

## Why it worked (seen vs guessed)
- **Seen:** BTS and result on screen together from 3s; a countdown hook; one take; the space
  changing about every 10s; a human mistake; a clear payoff.
- **Guessed:** a premium venue plus a dollar figure fed shares ("share this with someone you'd
  like to go with"). Shareability likely drove reach more than the hook did.

## Steal / avoid
1. Start FPV split screens with a **countdown or "action" moment**: anticipation in under 2s.
2. Get the **"live person doing it" proof (split screen) on screen by 3s at the latest.**
   Test showing it from frame 1.
3. **Change the environment or visual every ≤10s**, even with no cuts.
4. **Keep the imperfect moments**. "Blew out a lot of stuff, but it's okay" makes it human.
5. **Put a concrete stake** (a dollar figure, "1 take", a deadline) in on-screen text or the
   post caption.
6. Export at −14 LUFS, not −16.
