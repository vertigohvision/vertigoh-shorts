| Time | Beat |

## Retention devices
- **One open loop held for 30s** ("which county?"). He delays the answer while zooming the map
  toward Florida, so viewers can guess along.
- **Visuals follow his narration exactly.** The screen zooms state by state while he talks.
- **His vocal energy rises** toward the reveal.
- **He ends seconds after the payoff.** No outro, so it rewatches and loops.

## Captions

- The **keyword is set larger on the second line** ("2026", "huge shift", "blue arrow", "more

- Placed on the seam between screen and facecam. Changes with every phrase.

## Why it worked
- **Seen:** a clear question, a held reveal, a perfect match between narration and screen, a
  personality-driven payoff, and an instant ending.
- **Guessed:** topical (the day after the election) and slightly provocative, which drives
  comments.
- **The editing is minimal.** The structure comes from how he *talks* while streaming.

## Steal / avoid
1. **Two-line captions with the key word bigger** on line 2. A good middle ground between
   one-word pop and plain 3–5 word chunks. Add it as a third caption mode to test.
2. **"Everything is X… except one"** is a reusable hook-and-reveal shape for experiments and
   tool tests ("I tried 5 cameras, only one survived…").
3. **End within 2–3s of the payoff.** No outro, so it loops.
4. **Screen-share or BTS on top, face below** is the same grammar as Kai's FPV split screen.
   That layout is already proven for Kai's audience.
5. **For streaming later:** narrate while doing ("so if you zoom in…"). That's what makes a
   stream moment clippable without heavy editing.
