## Retention devices
- **Countdown** → open loop in under 2.5s ("what happens on ACTION?").
- **One take = constant tension.** Zero cuts detected (scene detection: 1 shot).
- **The space changes every ~6–10s:** lounge → mezzanine → crowd → dance floor → outdoors → aerial.
- **The bottom half switches** from a goggles close-up to a walking BTS cam to the close-up again,
  a pattern break that doesn't cut the FPV.
- **Live reaction payoff:** "Damn bro! That was pretty good" at 43–45s, right on the reveal.

- **Seen:** BTS proof from frame 0; a countdown hook; a one-take promise in the title; a fast-changing
  environment; a crowd/dance-floor spectacle at the midpoint; a live, honest reaction at the end; a short
  runtime (45s, vs 79s for the IG #1).

  dance-battle crowd adds broad appeal beyond FPV fans. The hashtags (#behindthescenes, #onetake)
  match a big, established BTS niche.

## Steal / avoid
1. **Split screen from frame 0, not 3s.** This one (10.7M) shows the pilot immediately.
2. **Put the stake in the title in 3 words or fewer** ("1 TAKE").
3. **Countdown → launch inside 2.5s.**
4. **A third camera on the pilot** (walking BTS) as a mid-video pattern break.
5. **End on a live reaction line** within ~1s of the reveal.
