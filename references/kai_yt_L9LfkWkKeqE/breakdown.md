## Why it worked
- **Seen:** pilot + drone on screen from frame 0; brand and superlative in the first sentence; a real
  problem stack (no-fly zone, no time); receipts on screen; a build beat (sub-250g drone) as the creative
  solution; fast cutting.
- **Guessed:** "Netflix" is a curiosity and authority magnet. The no-fly-zone problem is relatable to
  any drone owner. People watch to see whether he pulled it off, even though the title gives that away.

## Steal / avoid
1. **First sentence = "This is how I [big outcome] for [recognisable brand]".** Promise + authority by 4s.
2. **Stack 2–3 obstacles inside 10s** of the setup.
3. **Show the receipts** (texts, maps, emails, permits) as B-roll for each claim.
4. **Include a build/tech-solution beat**: that's the channel's differentiator.
5. **Avoid:** saving 12s of footage-only tail after the VO ends. Cut to ≤3s after the last line, or move
   the payoff earlier.
