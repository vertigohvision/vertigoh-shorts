## The current slump (key finding, 11/22)
- **Median views for the latest 30 shorts are 6.7K. For shorts 31–80 they're 36K, a 5× drop.**
- **The format mix shifted.** In the latest 40 shorts:

| Format | Shorts in latest 40 | Median views, latest 40 | Median views, older |
|---|---|---|---|
| FPV into/through a place, teams up, 1 take | **2** | 30K | 77K |
| Gear test / vs / review | 7 | 8.9K | 55K |
| Reflective / advice / career ("quit FPV?", "useless skill") | **18** | 4.75K | 25K |
| News (DJI ban) | 2 | 14K | 46K |

- **Two causes, both real:**
  1. **Format drift.** The best format almost disappeared, replaced by reflective content
     (the weakest format) and gear tests.
  2. **Every format also fell** in the recent window, so there's a channel-level decline too
     (distribution, audience fatigue, or packaging).
- **YouTube Studio data is needed to separate the two:** impressions, viewed vs swiped away,
  average % viewed.
